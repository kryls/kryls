KRYLS Protocol

KRYLS pairs a client–freelancer project app with non-custodial escrow for digital services. The app coordinates projects and collaboration; the protocol governs on-chain settlement, so the platform coordinates the work—not the money.

Work can be coordinated off-chain. Settlement can be enforced on-chain.

Project lifecycle

From agreement
to settlement.

The application supports the relationship. The escrow follows explicit project states and settlement conditions.

  1. 01

    Create

    The client sets project requirements, budget, asset, and engagement model.

  2. 02

    Connect

    Freelancers discover open projects and submit applications.

  3. 03

    Agree

    Both parties communicate, negotiate terms, and establish conditions.

  4. 04

    Fund

    After approval, the client creates and funds an escrow on Sepolia.

  5. 05

    Execute

    The freelancer performs the work and submits the result for review.

  6. 06

    Settle

    When the required conditions are met, the protocol executes settlement.

When a project is contested

Disputes have a structured path.

The current in-app dispute workflow is an MVP/off-chain component. The escrow architecture is prepared for Kleros-compatible flows; this does not mean live decentralized arbitration is active.
